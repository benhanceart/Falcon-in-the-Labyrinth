Spirit Releasement Session

These are CB Scott Jones' personal recordings of his Spirit Releasement sessions with Bill and Judith Baldwin. The Baldwins had been working with the Human Potential Foundation doing their spirit releasement work and workshops with the HPF members. The first tape was made by Judith Baldwin for CB Scott Jones after her psychic intuition showed her a problem with Scott. Then, the next tapes are Scott undergoing the spirit releasement process with Bill and Judith Baldwin at Jones' home in Falls Church, Virginia. Bill uses hypnosis on Scott as Judith uses her intuition to psychically view Scott and make suggestions to Bill. Scott's wife "Johnnie" joins after having undergone hypnosis as well. Her personal tape isn't included. The final tape from August, 11, 1993, is a recording Judith Baldwin made for Scott Jones expressing her feeling that all trust had been broken within the Human Potential Foundation. She makes reference to Scott and his HPF coworker's (Menalika McCarthy) extramarital affair as a source for her feeling. Scott also refers to these sessions in his call with Donna Rice Bassett later.
