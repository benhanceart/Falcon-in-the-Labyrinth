JY Ranch Meeting 1993

Here is the tape recording of the famous UFO roundtable meeting at Laurance Rockefeller's JY Ranch in Wyoming, September 13th-15th of 1993. There is an introduction by Scott Jones and Laurance Rockefeller. Then, presentations by Leo Sprinkle, Bruce Maccabee, Linda M. Howe, Steven Greer, Keith Thompson, Laurance Rockefeller, John Mack, Robert Bigelow, and Bootsie Galbraith. In the photo we have (From left to right): Henry Diamond, Steven Greer, George Lamb, Bruce Maccabee, Robert Bigelow, Linda Howe, Leo Sprinkle, Keith Thompson, John Mack, Laurance Rockefeller, CB Scott Jones, Marylin "Johnnie" Jones (Scott's wife), and Bob Teets. Seemingly Dick Farley and Bootsie Galbraith were behind the camera. (Some notes: In their correspondence, Rockefeller thanks Scott for the "Aviary Packet" and the photo from the meeting. The "Johnnie Jones Initiation into the Aviary" tape also says it was "inspired by the September 14-15, 1993 JY Ranch UFO Roundtable - Moose, WY." It was known in the Jones family that his wife often typed his letters and papers for him because his spelling wasn't great. He wanted to include her in the secrecy because she was already reading their letters to each other. She was reluctantly included in his work around that time. We also see from the Aviary files that Jones was given the name "Falcon" by his Aviary contacts, Bill Moore and Jamie Shandera. He was later sent a VHS tape of the Richard Doty Falcon Interview, with Moore and Shandera, and was avoided by them afterwards. Scott makes a reference to an Inner and Outer circle during the JY meeting. The only person he made a distinction about being on either side was John Mack was on the Outer Circle. The inner circle is never defined either. It could mean the Aviary members or possibly the HPF members that set up the meeting. Also, Linda Howe mentions information that came from Richard Doty. Since then, she has publicly expressed her distrust of Doty.)
